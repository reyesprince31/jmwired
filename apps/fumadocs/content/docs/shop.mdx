Open [the JMWired shop](http://127.0.0.1:3001/shop) to find network equipment, CCTV, office supplies, and local services. You can browse without signing in.

## Find an item

Choose **Networking**, **CCTV & security**, **Office essentials**, or **Local services** to narrow the list. Use **Search the shop** to look for an item or a task, such as CAT6, MikroTik, printing, or registration. Search applies to the selected category; choose **All items** to search the whole shop.

Select an item or **View details** to see its options. Photos illustrate the product category. Your team will confirm the exact model, included equipment, availability, and price before you agree to anything.

## Prepare a quote inquiry

1. Select **Request a quote** on an item's page, or **Build an inquiry** at the bottom of the shop.
2. Check **Product or service**, and enter the quantity or number of locations.
3. Enter your name and phone number. Email is optional.
4. Describe what you need: cable length, camera count, installation location, or the task you need help with.
5. Select **Prepare inquiry** and review the summary.
6. Select **Download inquiry** to keep a text copy and share it with your local team through your usual contact channel.

Preparing an inquiry does not place an order or take a payment. Equipment prices, installation scope, document requirements, and any official registration fees need confirmation from the team.

## Keep your copy

The app saves the most recently prepared inquiry on this device when browser storage is available. Preparing another inquiry replaces that saved copy. Download each inquiry you want to keep; the download includes its reference and the details you entered.

<Callout title="Current availability">
Online inquiry delivery is not connected in this preview. Use sample contact details when trying the form, and keep passwords, identity documents, and payment details out of it. If browser storage is unavailable, you can still download your prepared inquiry.
</Callout>
