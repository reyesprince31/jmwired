## Maintenance

**Start maintenance** requires confirmation. It shows a customer maintenance page and pauses new connection applications. Staff and super-admin workspaces remain open. **End maintenance** restores the portal.

## Registration

Uncheck **Allow new registrations and applications** to pause operator sign-up and public branch requests. Existing workspaces remain available.

## AI setup

Toggle **Enable AI assistance in staff inboxes** to show or hide summary and suggestion tools. Choose provider, model, and monthly token cap, then **Save AI settings**. Usage stays zero until provider reporting is connected.

Use a placeholder **API key**. Only a configured status is saved; the key value is discarded. Real credentials, requests, and usage enforcement remain setup work.

Platform changes are recorded in the audit log.
