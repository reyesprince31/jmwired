title: "Access your account"
description: "Sign in, activate a customer account, or recover access."
---

## Sign in

Open [Sign in](http://127.0.0.1:3001/sign-in), enter an email and a placeholder password of at least eight characters, and choose **Organization team**, **Customer portal**, or **Super admin**. Select **Sign in** to open that workspace. This selector explores the preview; it does not grant or enforce real permissions.

## Register an operator or staff member

Open [Create account](http://127.0.0.1:3001/sign-up), enter your name and email, choose **Branch owner** or **Invited staff member**, and use a placeholder password. Owners continue to organization creation. Staff will need a membership invitation from their branch owner when live sign-in is connected.

## Activate an existing subscription

Ask your provider for an activation code. Open [Activate account](http://127.0.0.1:3001/activate), enter the code, and check the displayed subscriber name and service address. Enter an email and matching placeholder passwords, then select **Activate account**. The record becomes Activated and the portal opens for that subscriber. Invalid or already used codes cannot be claimed.

Portal registration is optional: staff can still manage subscriptions and cash payments.

## Recover a password

Select **Forgot password?**, enter your email, and select **Send recovery link**. The preview shows a confirmation and a link to the reset screen; it sends no email. Enter matching new passwords on the reset screen.

Live recovery will require a valid email token. Never use a real password in this preview.
