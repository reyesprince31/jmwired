---
title: "Publish service updates"
description: "Tell affected subscribers about maintenance and outages."
---

![Post a customer update form with update type, audience, title, message, and Publish update](/help/publish-update.png)

1. Open **Announcements → Post update**.
2. Choose **Announcement**, **Maintenance**, or **Outage** under **Update type**.
3. Choose an **Audience**: **All customers**, **Service area · [area name]**, or one subscriber.
4. Enter a **Title** and **Message**. Include the affected area, expected interruption, and when your team will give another update.
5. For maintenance, enter **Maintenance starts (Asia/Manila)**.
6. Select **Publish update**.

The update appears immediately for its audience in the portal's **Updates** page and bell where relevant. Active maintenance and outage notices also appear on the overview. A maintenance start time is displayed information, not a delayed publication timer.

Service-area selection uses the subscribers currently assigned to that area. One subscriber selection sends the notice only to that account. Check the audience list after publishing, especially when several service areas share a branch.

After repair work, select **Mark resolved** on the maintenance or outage notice. The notice remains in history with **Resolved** status. Publish a follow-up if customers need details about the repair.

Device push is awaiting connection; current updates appear inside the portal. A notice does not perform a network repair or change an account's administrative status.
