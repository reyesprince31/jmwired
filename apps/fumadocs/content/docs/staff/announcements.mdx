---
title: "Publish service updates"
description: "Tell affected subscribers about maintenance and outages."
---

Open **Announcements → New**. Choose **Announcement**, **Maintenance**, or **Outage**, write a title and message, and add a schedule where relevant.

Publish to the whole branch or selected subscribers. For a smaller service area, choose the subscribers from that area's customer list. The update appears in their portal overview, Updates page, and bell where relevant.

After repair work, mark the outage resolved so customers can see restoration status. Device push is awaiting connection; current updates appear inside the portal.
