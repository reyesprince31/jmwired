Open **Support inbox**, search a subject or reference, and filter by status or assignee. Choose a conversation to work on it.

![Staff support conversation with assignment, status, reply suggestions, and message visibility controls](/help/staff-support.png)

## Assignment

Set **Ticket assignee**, **Ticket priority**, and **Ticket status**. Active branch members are available; pending invitations are excluded. Assign technicians line checks and repairs, then use the assignee filter to see their work.

## Replies and notes

Choose **Public reply** for the subscriber, or **Internal staff note** for the team. Internal notes are excluded from customer conversations and notifications. Select **Send reply** or **Save internal note**. A public reply moves an Open ticket to In progress.

Use internal notes for equipment to bring, visit findings, and staff handovers. Before sending a customer update, confirm **Message visibility** is set to **Public reply**. The visibility choice applies to the next message, including a suggested response.

## AI assistance

**Summarize** shows a conversation overview. **Draft quick response** puts a suggestion into the composer for editing. **Send suggested reply** sends it immediately. Check reply visibility before sending.

Preview suggestions use templates, including an acknowledgement for an active outage. Platform settings can hide AI tools. Real AI services are awaiting connection.

Check the customer's issue, repair status, and any timing in the response before sending. If the tools are missing, ask the platform administrator to check **System & AI**; ordinary replies still work.

## Reply automation

Open **Reply automation**, enable it, choose **Outages** or **Off-hours**, and enter a message. Use **Preview reply**, then **Save automation**.

Outage replies apply only to affected subscribers. Off-hours means before 8 AM or after 5 PM, Philippine time. One acknowledgement is added to new requests; existing tickets are unchanged.
