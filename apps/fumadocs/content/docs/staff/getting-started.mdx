---
title: "Set up your branch"
description: "Prepare the workspace, collections, team, and subscriber records."
---

Each organization is a separate branch or service area. Choose the organization in the sidebar before changing records. A new branch starts with its own subscribers, payments, expenses, support requests, and updates.

![Branch overview with the organization selector, operational navigation, and top-right user menu](/help/branch-overview.png)

## 1. Create or select a branch

Open the organization selector and choose **+ Create organization** to add a location. Enter the business name, service area, and a unique workspace address. Select **Create organization** to open its settings.

For an existing branch, select it from the same selector. Confirm the location shown before continuing.

## 2. Complete branch details

Open **Organization**. Add service areas, contact phone, email, and office hours, then select **Save workspace details**. These details help your team identify the branch and contact information.

## 3. Prepare collection instructions

Open **Billing & payments → Collection QR settings**. Configure each method you want customers to see, attach its QR image, enable it, and save it. [Follow the QR guide](/docs/staff/qr-settings).

In the current preview, use sample images and fictional destination details. Collection-account verification is performed outside the portal.

## 4. Add your team

Open **Organization → Invite member**. Enter a name, email, and role. Use Billing for collections, Technician for field work, or Support for conversations. [Review all roles](/docs/staff/organization#team-membership).

Invitations remain pending in the preview; email delivery and permission enforcement are awaiting connection. Pending invitees cannot be assigned tickets.

## 5. Add subscribers or review applications

Use **Customers → Add customer** for existing subscriptions. Record the plan, billing day, service address, contact details, and installation status. Use **Installation requests** for new households applying through the public form.

Portal registration is optional. Your team can manage billing and cash collections for a subscriber without an activated portal account.

## 6. Check the customer experience

Open the top-right user dropdown and choose **Customer portal**. The preview's **Account** selector lets you inspect a subscriber in the selected branch. Check the bill, collection instructions, announcements, and support history. This selector is for reviewing sample accounts; live access control is still to be connected.

## Keep the branch running

Use [Daily branch work](/docs/staff/daily-work) for collections, support, and updates. Router readings and device push notifications are separate integration work; their setup status is explained under [Network & usage](/docs/staff/network).
