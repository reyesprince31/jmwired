---
title: "Collection QR settings"
description: "Configure payment instructions for this branch."
---

1. Open **Billing & payments → Collection QR settings**.
2. Enter the account name and number for GCash, Maya, or Bank.
3. Attach the verified collection QR image (PNG, JPEG, or WebP up to 1 MB).
4. Add instructions, such as including a subscriber account number.
5. Check **Show this method to customers** and select the method's **Save** button.

An enabled method requires a QR attachment. The customer bill displays that branch's saved destination. Uncheck and save to stop showing a destination.

Use sample images and fictional account details while exploring. The preview does not verify QR destinations or transfer money.
