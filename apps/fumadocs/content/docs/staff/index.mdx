---
title: "Manage your local branch"
description: "Select the right workspace before updating records."
---

Use the organization selector to switch branches. Branch links include an address such as [Poblacion](http://127.0.0.1:3001/organization/poblacion) or [San Jose](http://127.0.0.1:3001/organization/san-jose). Shared customer and ticket links open the matching branch.

The overview shows active customers, pending proofs, open tickets, and monthly clients, paid accounts, collections, expenses, and net profit.

Open your profile dropdown and choose **Customer portal** to view the selected branch’s customer experience. **Help & guides** in the sidebar opens this help center.
