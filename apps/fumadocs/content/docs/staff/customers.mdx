---
title: "Subscribers and activation"
description: "Manage self-service and staff-managed accounts."
---

## Add a subscriber

Select **Customers → Add customer**. Enter name, phone, service address and area, plan, billing day, installation date, status, and notes. Email is optional. Select **Pending installation** for unfinished connections, **Active** for connected accounts, or **Suspended** for stopped connections.

## Update accounts

Search by name, account, or email; filter by status and area. **View account** opens usage, history, and subscriber details. Edit fields and internal notes, then **Save subscriber details**. Notes are staff-only.

**Suspend connection** and **Reactivate connection** update the saved status. For a pending installation, reactivate after the connection is installed.

## Cash collection

After collecting cash, use **Mark paid · Cash** in the directory or ledger, or **Record cash payment** in an account. The bill becomes Paid. Review an existing pending digital proof before recording another collection.

## Optional portal access

Portal status can be **Unregistered**, **Invited**, or **Activated**. **Generate new activation code** creates or replaces a code. Share it with the subscriber, or use **Open activation page** to explore the claim process. Activation consumes the code.

Staff can manage subscribers who never register for the portal.
