description: "Find the next step when a payment, account, or request needs attention."

## My payment is still pending

Open **Billing → View submitted proof**. Check that the reference and screenshot match your transfer. **Pending review** means staff have not approved it yet; uploading proof does not automatically mark the bill paid.

Do not send another payment just to clear the status. Contact your provider through **Support**, including your account number and payment reference. The portal does not promise a review deadline.

## My proof was rejected

Read the reviewer note in the payment details. Return to **Billing → Pay with QR code**, enter the correct reference, and attach a corrected screenshot. A rejected proof remains in your history. [See the payment guide](/docs/customer/billing).

## I cannot upload a screenshot

Use a PNG, JPEG, or WebP image no larger than 1 MB. A PDF or another file format will not be accepted. Save or export a smaller image if necessary, keeping the amount, recipient, transaction reference, and transfer result readable.

**Submit payment proof** becomes available when a screenshot has finished loading and the transaction reference has at least four characters. Check the image preview before submitting.

## There is no collection QR

If the bill says **Collection QR awaiting setup**, contact your provider for verified payment instructions. The placeholder icon is not a collection code.

## My activation code does not work

Confirm the code with your provider. An already used or replaced code cannot be claimed again. Staff can generate a new code for the correct subscriber. Do not activate an account if the displayed name or service address belongs to someone else.

## I do not receive phone notifications

Billing and outage preferences are available in **Account**, and notices appear inside the portal. Device push delivery is awaiting connection. Enabling a preference in the preview does not produce background phone notifications.

## My connection is not working

Read **Updates** for an outage or maintenance notice, then [report the problem](/docs/customer/support) if you need help. Include when it started, whether all devices are affected, and the lights shown on your equipment. Avoid opening duplicate requests for the same ongoing issue.

## My request is marked resolved but I still need help

Open the existing conversation and send a follow-up describing what remains wrong. The conversation stays in your history. Staff can review the follow-up and update the status.

## The portal shows “We’ll be back shortly”

Your provider has enabled portal maintenance. Contact the local office for urgent account or connection help. The help center does not show a live restoration estimate.
