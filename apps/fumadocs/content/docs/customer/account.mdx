Select your avatar to see your name, account number, email, service address, organization, and connection status. Ask your provider to correct connection details.

## Notifications

1. Select your avatar and find **Notification preferences**.
2. Enable or disable **Billing reminders** and **Maintenance and outage alerts**.
3. Select **View billing reminder** or **View maintenance alert** to inspect the corresponding message.

Preferences save as you change them; there is no separate Save button. You can also use **Overview → Manage notifications** to open these controls.

![Notification preferences with billing reminders, maintenance and outage alerts, and message preview buttons](/help/notification-preferences.png)

The preview shows messages inside the portal. Background phone push alerts are awaiting connection. [Service updates](/docs/customer/updates) explains the bell and notices.

## Password and devices

The page also includes password and session screens. Use a current placeholder and matching new placeholder passwords. Leave **Sign out other devices** checked to remove the other sample device.

Use **Sign out device** for other devices, or **Sign out of this device** to return to sign-in. Actual password verification and session revocation are awaiting connection.

For a wrong email, address, plan, or account status, [send a request to your provider](/docs/customer/support). These subscription details are not editable from the customer account page.
