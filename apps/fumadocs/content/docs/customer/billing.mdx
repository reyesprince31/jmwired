## Submit proof

![Customer billing screen showing the current bill, QR payment steps, and payment history](/help/customer-billing.png)

Check the **Current bill** amount, due date, and status first. A paid bill shows a zero balance and **View payment details**. A pending proof shows **View submitted proof** instead of another payment button.

1. Open **Billing**, check the balance and due date, and select **Pay with QR code**.
2. Choose **GCash**, **Maya**, or **Bank**.
3. Check the collection account, QR image, and instructions. If details are awaiting setup, ask your provider for a verified destination.
4. Complete payment in your payment app and save its screenshot.
5. Enter the transaction reference and attach the screenshot (PNG, JPEG, or WebP, up to 1 MB).
6. Select **Submit payment proof**.

The bill becomes **Pending review**. Do not pay again while staff check the transfer.

![Payment proof form showing the payment method, collection details, transaction reference, screenshot attachment, and Submit payment proof button](/help/payment-proof-form.png)

_The attachment preview and transaction reference are checked before submission. This example branch has not configured its collection QR yet._

In the current preview, use sample images and references. No payment app is connected, and the portal does not make a transfer.

## Correct a rejected proof

Open the rejected payment's **View details** and read the reviewer note. Return to **Billing**, choose **Pay with QR code**, and submit a corrected screenshot and reference. The earlier rejected submission remains in your history.

If you already sent money but cannot identify the problem, contact your provider through **Support** before making another transfer.

## Payment history

Open **Payment history → View details** to inspect the billing period, amount, method, reference, screenshot, and reviewer note.

Payment history appears on the **Billing** page. The detail view also shows the reviewer when an approved payment has one recorded.

| Status         | What it means                                  |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Pending review | Staff are checking the transfer                |
| Paid           | Staff approved proof or recorded cash          |
| Rejected       | Read the note, correct the proof, and resubmit |

For cash, pay an authorized cashier. Staff record the collection and it appears in your history. JMWired records payments; it does not transfer money.

If the current bill shows **Pending installation**, wait for your provider to activate the connection before billing is available. For upload errors or missing QR instructions, see [Common questions](/docs/customer/troubleshooting).
