## Submit proof

1. Open **Billing**, check the balance and due date, and select **Pay with QR code**.
2. Choose **GCash**, **Maya**, or **Bank**.
3. Check the collection account, QR image, and instructions. If details are awaiting setup, ask your provider for a verified destination.
4. Complete payment in your payment app and save its screenshot.
5. Enter the transaction reference and attach the screenshot (PNG, JPEG, or WebP, up to 1 MB).
6. Select **Submit payment proof**.

The bill becomes **Pending review**. Do not pay again while staff check the transfer.

## Payment history

Open **Payment history → View details** to inspect the billing period, amount, method, reference, screenshot, and reviewer note.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Pending review | Staff are checking the transfer |
| Paid | Staff approved proof or recorded cash |
| Rejected | Read the note, correct the proof, and resubmit |

For cash, pay an authorized cashier. Staff record the collection and it appears in your history. JMWired records payments; it does not transfer money.
