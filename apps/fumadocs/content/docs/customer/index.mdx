Start at [Customer overview](http://127.0.0.1:3001/portal). It shows your plan, connection status, current balance, saved usage, active notices, and open requests.

Use **Billing**, **Support**, and **Updates** in the header, or the bottom navigation on a phone. Select the account avatar for account and notification settings.
