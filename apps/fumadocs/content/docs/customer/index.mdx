Start at [Customer overview](http://127.0.0.1:3001/portal). Check the name, account number, and service area shown. The overview brings together your plan, connection status, current bill, usage, service notices, and open requests.

![Customer overview showing the plan, account number, current bill, data usage, and support requests](/help/customer-overview.png)

_Example account: the bill and usage appear together on Overview._

Use **Billing**, **Support**, and **Updates** in the header, or the bottom navigation on a phone. Select the account avatar for account and notification settings.

| If you need to…                           | Open…                                  |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Check your amount due or payment result   | Billing                                |
| Ask about a connection or account problem | Support                                |
| Read maintenance and outage notices       | Updates                                |
| Change reminder preferences               | Your avatar → Notification preferences |

If you have not activated your account, ask your provider for an activation code and follow [Account access](/docs/access). Staff can also manage your subscription without portal registration.
