Open **Overview** in your customer portal. Check the account number and service area before reading the figures.

## Your plan

The plan panel shows your plan name and speed in **Mbps**. Mbps describes a connection's data rate; the **GB** shown under Data usage describes the amount of data used. They measure different things.

The current plans display **Unlimited data**. The usage figure is informational and does not represent a remaining data allowance.

## Data usage

**Data usage** shows a total for this billing cycle, a seven-day chart, and separate download and upload figures. Download refers to data received by your connection; upload refers to data sent from it.

In this preview, the total is a saved sample reading. The daily bars and download/upload split are illustrative. They do not measure your router or run a speed test. Live reporting will be documented when connected.

## Connection status

| Status               | What to do                                                                                                |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Online               | No active outage notice is recorded for this account. Report a problem if your connection is not working. |
| Outage               | Read the active notice and follow updates from your provider.                                             |
| Suspended            | Contact the provider to confirm the reason and reactivation requirements.                                 |
| Pending installation | Ask your local team about the installation schedule. Billing is not yet available.                        |

The displayed status comes from the account record and published notices. It is not a live check of your equipment in the preview.

## Question a reading or plan detail

Create a [support request](/docs/customer/support) with your account number, the figure or plan detail you are asking about, and when you noticed it. Staff can review your account. The customer portal does not offer a plan-change control yet.
