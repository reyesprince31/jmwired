## Find an answer

Use **Search** to look for a task or a phrase shown on your screen, such as “payment proof” or “activation code.” On a phone, open the navigation menu to browse the guides. Each article has links to related tasks.

For help with your own bill, connection, or installation schedule, [send your local team a support request](/docs/customer/support). This help center explains how to use the portal; your provider handles account decisions and repairs.

The current app is an interactive preview. [Read what is available](/docs/preview) before entering account details or credentials.
